WHOAMI

I'm Carlos Eduardo, a cybersecurity professional who came from infrastructure. I know how environments are built, and that is exactly how I know where they break.

My foundation is infrastructure: routing and switching with MikroTik and pfSense, VLAN segmentation, VPN, Linux and Windows Server, Active Directory, Samba file servers and virtualization on Proxmox. That is the ground where every attack and every defense actually happens.

On top of that foundation I build my own labs to practice offensive security in a controlled way: reconnaissance, enumeration, privilege escalation and Active Directory attack paths. Then I switch sides: I collect the logs, build the detection, investigate what happened and harden the environment.

I don't run tools blindly. I want to understand why an attack works, what it leaves behind and how to stop it. Every lab here ends with documentation and a repository.
